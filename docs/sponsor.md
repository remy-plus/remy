# Sponsor Remy

Remy is an open-source AI agent that does real digital work through one chat box. You say what you need, and it researches, works with your files and apps, and asks before it acts. It runs on your own machine or server, with any model you choose.

I am building it alone and working toward 1.0, a polished web app.

## What exists today

- **Live:** on [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koryei/remy) and [GitHub](https://github.com/remy-plus/remy), Apache-2.0, 9 public releases in 10 days
- **Built so far:** a browser it controls, connections to GitHub and Notion, memory, skills, helpers, schedules
- **Tested:** more than 1,600 automated tests and 83 replayed scenarios, with CI passing
- **Safety in code:** after reading anything untrusted, Remy asks before it acts, and an approval covers one exact action
- **Measured:** results are [public](evals.md), including what is not proven yet

## Where it is going

The [road to 1.0](../PLAN.md) has nine stages: a hard public test suite, better intelligence, deeper memory, voice, a designed web app, real-world testing and launch. 1.0 will run on a laptop or a home server, with your data staying yours.
