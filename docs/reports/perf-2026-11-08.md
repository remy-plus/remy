## Slowest parts

- The 10 slowest replay cases: `trust-long-run-crash-and-pause` 53 ms (63 steps), `hard-long-rename-twenty` 16 ms, `memory-5000-notes-bounded-prompt` 11 ms, `research-bridge-year` 10 ms, `hard-long-twelve-month-files` 6 ms, `conn-read-trusted-no-approval` 6 ms, and four cases at 4 ms.
- Start-up from source: a CPU profile of `--help` shows about 23 percent in Node's TypeScript stripping (the amaro wasm module) and about 20 percent in Node's own module loader. Remy's own code does not stand out. The source path costs about 50 ms more than the bundle.
- The full `pnpm check` does not finish green on this checkout. Biome fails on the untracked `.agents/` folder (third-party skill assets), not on committed code. I did not change it. The owner should decide whether to ignore or remove that folder.

## Findings

Start-up is about 0.14 s and about 124 MB resident for the shipped bundle. Replay runs a whole case in about 1 ms. Nothing is slow enough to justify a rewrite.

## Recommendation

Keep shipping the bundled CLI, and leave the code alone. If a gain is ever wanted, lazy-load the rarely used command modules (webhooks, MCP, daemon), but the profile does not point at any of them yet, so measure first.
